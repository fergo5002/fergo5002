## Hi, I'm Fergus

Engineer in Dublin. I like sitting with the people who'll use the thing, working out what's actually wrong, and shipping the fix.

Right now I'm a founding engineer on something new that isn't ready to talk about. Before that I co-founded [Tigh Sauna](https://tighsauna.com) and was a founding engineer at Loira AI. I study Computer Science and Business at Trinity in whatever time is left.

That's my website booting up there. [Go and turn it on](https://fergusoreilly.dev). There's an arcade behind the glass.

**Things I've made**

- [**voicesmith**](https://github.com/fergo5002/voicesmith): clone a voice on your own laptop, with the owner's permission, and let your AI agent talk in it. Watermarks everything.
- [**Cinematic Film Kit**](https://github.com/fergo5002/cinematic-film-kit): short films made entirely in code, with a coding agent doing the editing.
- [**motion-gauge**](https://github.com/fergo5002/motion-gauge): counts how much is moving on a web page, and refuses to answer if the browser can't measure properly.
- [**Resource Queue**](https://github.com/fergo5002/resource-queue): a job queue that learns what your builds and tests cost, then runs together only what fits.
- [**FergusOS**](https://github.com/fergo5002/fergus-portfolio): the site above. A CRT, some useful tools, three arcade cabinets.
- [**RLPong**](https://github.com/fergo5002/RLPong): Pong with a boost meter.
- [**Contra**](https://github.com/fergo5002/contraTradingBot): built to trade against r/wallstreetbets, with paper money. It doesn't trade yet, and the README says why.

Hackathons, with great teams: [**Specter**](https://github.com/cronneln/DaytonaGiveAGoHackathon) watched what npm packages really do inside cloud sandboxes, and [**Remand**](https://github.com/georgelevins/Nybblers) dug product ideas out of online discussions.

Otherwise: tennis, mountains or the sea.
